What makes up a Commercial HVAC System?

Commercial HVAC systems have many essential components. The first are the components that keep the air circulating. This includes the blower, ductwork, and vents. Additionally, there are components that regulate the air temperature. These include the air handler, evaporator coil, and condenser. Finally, there are components that monitor the system and make sure it is operating efficiently. These include the thermostat, filters, and compressors. All of these elements work together to ensure your HVAC system is running at peak efficiency.

What Is The Expected Lifespan Of A Commercial Air Conditioning System?+

When it comes to commercial air conditioning systems, one of the most important things to consider is their expected lifespan. How long can you expect your system to last? As a general rule, these systems typically have an average life expectancy of around 15-20 years depending on how well they are maintained and serviced.

It’s worth noting that regular maintenance and servicing will help extend the life of any air conditioning system. This includes making sure all components work properly, checking for signs of wear or damage, and changing out filters when necessary. By staying on top of routine maintenance you’ll be able to maximize the life span of your air conditioning unit and ensure that it continues working efficiently year after year.

With proper care and maintenance, you can rest assured that your commercial air conditioning system will provide reliable cooling performance for many years to come.
